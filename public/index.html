⚙️ How to hook up your real accounts

  1. AI (already set up if the chip is green). The app uses Mistral and falls back to OpenRouter. Keys go in your .env file: MISTRAL_API_KEY / OPEN_ROUTER_API_KEY.
  2. Stripe (real money in). Grab your secret key from dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys, put it in .env as STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, restart. Use sk_test_… to play safely, sk_live_… for real payments.
  3. Cash App. Put your $cashtag in .env as CASHAPP_CASHTAG. That's all Cash App allows — a payment link.

⚠️ Reality check: no app can legally "connect and generate money by itself." Money comes from someone paying you. This app helps you get those payments fast. Anything promising auto-profit from your Cash App is a scam.
