DOOM 2D

Top-down demon-blasting arena combat. Clear waves, earn credits, buy your way into sealed rooms, and rescue prisoners to build a squad that fights at your side.

MOBILE: Left thumb = MOVE  •  Right thumb = AIM + AUTO-FIRE
DESKTOP: WASD / Arrows = move  •  Mouse = aim  •  Click/Space = shoot
1–5 switch weapons  •  B buy room  •  H heal  •  Tab AI auto-pilot
Walk into a glowing door barrier + press BUY to open a room. Touch a green prisoner to rescue.
Tip: turn on AI mode anytime to let the auto-pilot take over and fight for you.
