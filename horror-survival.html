HOLLOW CREEK

Your car died on the forest road. The only lights you saw were the gates of Camp Hollow Creek — abandoned since the children vanished in '86. The gate locked itself behind you.

Restore power to open it: find the rusty key, unlock the chapel, collect 3 fuses from the buildings, and start the generator. Then run.
